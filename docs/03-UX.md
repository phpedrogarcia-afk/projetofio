# 03 — Experience and interface

Status: Canonical direction for specification v0.1

Platform note: `ADR-033` changes the initial client to Android. The experience,
copy, accessibility, privacy-cover, and app-lock outcomes remain canonical.
Apple-specific lifecycle/authentication assumptions are Superseded. Android
Compose, TalkBack, `BiometricPrompt`, `FLAG_SECURE`, and Recents protections are
accepted through `ADR-034` and `ADR-035`; device/API coverage remains gated by
`ADR-036` and the Android M1 plan.

## Experience goal

Fio should feel like a quiet, well-made personal object rather than a technology
dashboard. It may be beautiful, but its beauty frames the user's words instead
of competing with them.

The product should feel smaller than the user's life.

## Visual language

- Warm off-white surfaces, sage green accents, soft charcoal text.
- Restrained botanical detail in peripheral or empty states, never over journal
  content.
- Elegant serif may be used for the name and short headings.
- Journal body, editor text, controls, and accessibility labels use a highly
  legible typeface. No cursive body text.
- Generous whitespace and quiet hierarchy.
- Subtle transitions, typically 150–300 ms, with a complete Reduce Motion path.
- No large lock, envelope, vault, confetti, or reward animation after saving.

Color must never be the only carrier of state. All text and controls must meet
platform contrast, Android font-scaling, TalkBack, and touch-target
expectations.

## Information architecture

Primary navigation:

1. **Guardar** — opening and primary surface.
2. **Encontrar** — deliberate retrieval of the user's original words.
3. **Arquivo** — complete chronological history; calm and non-algorithmic.

Secondary/global surfaces:

4. **Return** — opened from one quiet notification or from a pending local
   return state.
5. **Settings** — privacy, app lock, return controls, import, export, deletion.

Avoid a dashboard, activity feed, statistics home, mood chart, or prominent
streak. Guardar remains the opening surface and receives the strongest content
hierarchy. Encontrar and Arquivo must still be discoverable in one tap; their
secondary role is expressed through quieter content, never obscure gestures or
overflow-only navigation.

Settings uses progressive disclosure. Its first page is a short map organized
by what the person wants to do, not by internal milestones or modules. Each
destination explains the consequence before showing controls. Never expose
labels such as M1/M2/M3, engineering, validation mode, schema, worker or local
flags as user-facing copy.

Opening an Archive row must visibly lead to a reading detail where Edit and
Delete are available. Do not repeat destructive buttons in every row, but do
not require the person to guess that the row is actionable.

## Write flow

Canonical PT-BR prompt:

> O que está passando pela sua cabeça hoje?

Requirements:

- text field is immediately available and readable;
- no required title, tags, mood, topic, or template;
- exactly one active draft is protected locally with the same encrypted-content
  path as Entries;
- meaningful text changes autosave after a quiet debounce; blank drafts are not
  persisted;
- explicit action label: `Guardar`;
- save succeeds offline;
- `Guardar` clears the draft only after the Entry transaction succeeds;
- encryption or save failure preserves the recoverable draft, never shows
  `Guardado.`, and explains the next safe action;
- keyboard and screen-reader navigation are first-class.

The editor may support plain text first. Rich formatting is outside V0 unless
later evidence requires it.

## Saved state

Canonical confirmation:

> Guardado.

Optional secondary copy:

> Sua lembrança foi guardada.

The confirmation is immediate, brief, and visually modest. A small check can be
used. Do not imply a specific encryption property that has not been technically
verified. Do not turn saving into a ritual that rewards repeated use.

## Archive

The Archive is chronological, calm, and complete.

```text
Arquivo

2026
7 de agosto
31 de julho

2025
18 de dezembro
3 de novembro
```

Requirements:

- list by year and date, newest first by default;
- titles are optional; use a short local preview only when privacy settings
  permit it;
- editing, deletion, return controls, sealing, and history live in a restrained
  detail menu;
- search is user-initiated and visually distinct from unsolicited returns;
- recently deleted content is accessible from Settings or Archive management;
- no algorithmic ranking, popularity, or “memories you should read” feed.

## Return notification

Canonical notification:

> Algo seu voltou.

Rules:

- no entry text, topic, emotional label, date, person, photo, or audio preview;
- no sensational subtitle;
- no repeated reminder if ignored;
- no badge designed to create urgency;
- respect quiet hours and notification permission;
- the app must remain usable when notifications are disabled.

## Return screen

The return screen shows:

- the original date;
- original entry content;
- attachment only if the user explicitly included it and local authentication
  requirements are satisfied;
- a quiet way to close;
- an overflow menu for `Não mostrar novamente` and `Deixar descansar`.

It does not show:

- similarity scores, model names, topics, sentiment, or “why this returned”;
- generated summaries, advice, or claims of growth;
- celebratory illustration or a carousel of more content;
- another recommended entry by default.

The screen may include “há N dias/meses” only if it improves orientation without
competing with the original date.

## Pilot feedback

In research builds only, after the user closes the return, a separate and
skippable sheet may ask:

> Isso significou algo para você?

Options:

- `Sim`
- `Não`
- close/skip

The answer is never required to dismiss, rest, reject, or continue. Production
builds should be able to remove the sheet entirely through a feature flag.

## Return controls and language

Before Milestone 2 onboarding, the global Return state is `notConfigured` and
Fio delivers no Return. Onboarding must ask explicitly before changing it to
`enabled`; an existing Entry marked `eligible` is not consent. The control
`Pausar lembranças` becomes available only after configuration and transitions
the state to `paused` until the person explicitly resumes.

- `Não devolver esta nota novamente` changes this entry to Never Return only
  after confirmation. The copy states that the note remains in Archive and
  that this version does not offer reversal.
- `Deixar descansar` temporarily excludes the entry. The next date/window must
  be clear before confirmation; it is not a task reminder.
- `Lembrar depois` is a user-requested one-time return around a future date.
- `Pausar lembranças` applies globally and asks no retention question.
- `Selar esta lembrança` requires local authentication before viewing. Explain
  that sealing is an additional access barrier, not the definition of
  encryption.

Avoid therapeutic or omniscient language such as “healing,” “trigger,” “the
right time,” or “we understood how you feel.”

## App privacy behavior

- Hide private content in the app switcher using a neutral Fio surface; this
  privacy cover is enabled by default.
- Transient Android surfaces may pause Fio without stopping it. The cover must
  clear after a safe resume when the current access gate is already resolved,
  and must never reveal content while lock state is unresolved.
- App lock is optional and off until explicitly enabled. It supports immediate,
  one-minute, or five-minute grace periods when verified platform behavior
  permits.
- Use platform device-owner authentication, which may use biometrics or device
  passcode. Fio does not store or evaluate biometric information.
- A failed biometric attempt falls back according to platform policy; never
  invent custom security questions.
- Failed or cancelled authorization reveals no Entry preview. One successful
  authorization does not indefinitely authorize later protected actions.
- When app lock is enabled, require fresh approved authorization before export
  and permanent deletion.
- A sealed entry cannot appear in a notification or unblurred system preview.

## Settings language and progressive disclosure

Settings begins with a short map organized by human intent: privacy, memories,
and the person's data. Every destination explains its effect before showing a
control. Internal milestone, validation, batch, schema, engine, and engineering
terms must not appear in user-facing copy.

- Use **Lembranças que voltam** in the interface. “Return/devolução” remains a
  domain term and cannot be required knowledge for the person using the app.
- Use **Desfazer esta importação**, never “desfazer lote.”
- Export explains which format to choose: plain text is the simplest default;
  Markdown preserves more document structure for compatible applications.
- Permanent deletion explains both consequences in plain language: the note
  cannot be recovered in Fio, while device storage may retain temporary traces.
- A short footer states what the installed build does not provide yet, without
  presenting internal feature flags or validation-stage names.

## M1 export experience

Settings or Archive management offers two explicit choices: one combined
Markdown document or one combined plain-text document. Both are UTF-8, contain
active Entries oldest to newest, and preserve original timestamps, known
timezone/offset, and exact user content. Copy must explain that the selected
destination is outside Fio's protection. Export adds no title, topic, summary,
sentiment, or other inferred description and never uploads a copy.

## M3 local import experience

Import lives in Settings and is available only in the isolated validation
variant. The person selects one local TXT/Markdown document through Android's
document picker, authorizes access when app lock requires it, then sees a quiet
preview before any Archive change. The preview distinguishes new Entries,
exact duplicates, and typed errors; it shows the original date and exact words
without inferred titles, topics, summaries, or corrections.

`Importar` is an explicit commit and `Cancelar` leaves the Archive unchanged.
Committed batches remain visible as local provenance. A confirmed rollback
moves only unchanged batch-created Entries to Recently Deleted, preserves later
edits, and states exclusions plainly. Do not imply that every diary format is
supported, that parsing understands prose, or that Fio repaired ambiguous data.

## Empty, loading, and error states

Empty state copy must not pressure the user to write. Prefer:

> Quando quiser, suas palavras podem ficar aqui.

Loading should be rare because core flows are local. Sync status belongs in a
secondary location and must distinguish:

- saved locally;
- waiting to sync;
- synced;
- action required.

Never show “not saved” when an entry is safely saved locally but remote sync is
pending.

## Accessibility and emotional safety

- Support Android font scaling without clipping entry content or controls.
- TalkBack order follows date → content → actions.
- Reduce Motion removes non-essential transitions.
- Do not auto-play audio or reveal a photo before explicit opening.
- Return controls remain reachable without swipes only.
- Provide a fast exit from a return without asking for a reason.
- Pausing returns never disables access to the Archive.

## Copy test

Every sentence should pass three questions:

1. Does it sound like a quiet invitation rather than an instruction?
2. Does it claim more intelligence, security, or emotional certainty than the
   product can prove?
3. Could it make a person feel guilty for leaving?

If the answer to question 2 or 3 is yes, rewrite it.
